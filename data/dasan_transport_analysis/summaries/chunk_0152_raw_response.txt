## Summary

The analysis reveals that **Dasan call center's public transportation counseling is primarily time-focused (35%), with significant room for improvement in real-time information accessibility and decision-support guidance**. 

The most critical findings:

1. **Information Gaps**: Discount information and real-time scheduling require manual app/website referral, reducing customer satisfaction
2. **Decision Support Lacking**: Customers receive information but lack guidance for cost-convenience trade-offs
3. **Transfer Friction**: Multi-segment journeys (transfers) create psychological resistance despite being faster
4. **Segment Differentiation**: Travel scenarios require different counseling strategies than daily commute inquiries

**Primary Recommendations**:
- Integrate real-time discount/promotion data into counselor systems
- Develop decision-support frameworks (cost-focused vs time-focused routing)
- Create comparison templates for major routes (bus vs train vs transit)
- Implement faster schedule lookup systems to reduce wait times
